# Looks 2.0: Me tab design (Task 4)

Boards: Me dark / light / Arabic, Appearance, Profile and privacy, Delete my account. Both looks.
- **Me:** profile card (avatar, name, @username, visibility, level), grouped lists: Appearance (Look, Accent, Language, Theme), Data (History and backup, Profile and privacy, Download exercise pictures), About (Privacy Policy, Terms), then Sign out and Delete my account (red).
- **Appearance:** three look tiles with live miniature previews (Classic, Carbon, Bloom; the chosen one ticked), accent swatches per look plus a custom colour, language and theme segmented controls, "Large demo picture" switch.
- **Profile and privacy:** username and display name, Private / Friends / Public, find-by-e-mail switch, About you fields, Save, stop sharing.
- **Delete:** warning card, Download backup first, the six steps with progress ticks, Delete everything / Keep my account (tab bar hidden).
- **Classic data-act kept:** look-set, accent-set, lang-set, theme-set, demo-size-set, pics-download, pics-clear, goto-history, export, edit-profile, share-vis, share-email, share-delete(-yes/-no), delete-open, del-backup, del-start, del-reload, del-no, del-back, ask-del, signin (sign out).
